About Madzendi's Kitchen

Madzendi's Kitchen is a well-known street food stall located in Soweto, Gauteng. It is owned and operated by Mr. Tshifhiwa Munyai. The stall has been serving the local community for many years with freshly prepared South African street food.

Mr. Munyai sells a variety of popular South African street foods directly to customers from his stall. The business is not part of any chain or franchise — only freshly prepared food products made with love every day.
